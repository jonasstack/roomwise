# 3D models

`dog-shiba.glb`, `dog-husky.glb` — animated dogs by **Quaternius**
(<https://quaternius.com>, via <https://poly.pizza>), released under **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**.
Slimmed for the card (duplicate animations removed, vertex data quantized).

`car-hatch.glb`, `car-sedan.glb`, `car-suv.glb` — cars ("Normal Car 1/2", "SUV") by **Quaternius**
(via <https://poly.pizza>), **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**. Paint, lights and glass are restyled by the card.
